Dream Symbols

Motifs found in the dreams you have written down — places, scenarios, creatures, people, feelings. The more you record, the more the pattern shows.

These readings are common interpretations offered for your own reflection. They are not diagnosis, and they are not facts about you. What a symbol means in your dream is something only you can decide — the point of this page is to make the repetition visible, not to explain it away.
